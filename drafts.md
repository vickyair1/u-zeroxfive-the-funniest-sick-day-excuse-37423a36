# Drafts — The Funniest Sick-Day Excuse (my own original lines)

Judge criteria (from RULES.txt): half-believable + gets a laugh; fresh idea
(not fever/dog/dead grandmother); twist near the end; sounds like a real
person typing; no wasted words. One line, under 200 chars.

## Draft A — sneeze / back
Can't come in today — I sneezed so hard I pulled a muscle in my back, and now
I'm flat on the floor renegotiating my spine's contract.

## Draft B — dentist / numb face
Won't be in today — my dentist numbed half my face yesterday and the feeling
still hasn't clocked back in for work.

## Verdict — Draft A wins
- Believability: a sneeze straining your back is genuinely plausible, so a boss
  might half-believe it — strongest of the three on criterion 1.
- Freshness: not a fever/dog/grandmother excuse; the injury is absurd but
  mundane.
- Twist: "renegotiating my spine's contract" lands at the very end.
- Voice: "Can't come in today —" reads like a real text to a boss.
- Trim: "flat on the floor" does work (shows he really can't move); keep it.

## FINAL (revised wording)
