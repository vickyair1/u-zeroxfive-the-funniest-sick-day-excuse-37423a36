One line, under 200 characters, that someone could send to call in sick — the
funniest excuse in one breath, and nothing else.

[Bounty page](https://make.fun/bounty/u-zeroxfive-the-funniest-sick-day-excuse-37423a36)

## The line

> Can't come in today — I sneezed so hard I pulled a muscle in my back, and now I'm flat on the floor renegotiating my spine's contract.

134 characters. A sneeze straining your back is genuinely plausible, so a boss
might half-believe it — and the laugh lands at the very end, when the spine
gets a contract to renegotiate.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py   # writes /output/entry.txt, no trailing newline
```

Stdlib only, deterministic.
